The Casco Bay Crossing

A crisp, herbaceous highball that bridges the gap between summer greenery and autumn orchard fruit. The snap pea and rosemary profiles of the gin pair with dry cider, while a touch of elderflower and saline mimics a cool sea breeze.

PROFILE: Crisp Botanical Subtly Floral Effervescent

ELEMENTS IN THE GLASS

  • Bimini Gin
  • St-Germain Elderflower
  • Dry Artisanal Hard Cider
  • Fresh Lemon Juice
  • Rich Honey Syrup (2:1)
  • Sea Salt Saline
  • Garnish: Fresh Rosemary & Crisp Green Apple

INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING

  • 1.5 oz Bimini Gin (or hop-and-citrus forward modern gin)
  • 0.5 oz St-Germain Elderflower Liqueur
  • 0.5 oz Fresh lemon juice
  • 0.25 oz Rich honey syrup (2:1 honey to warm water)
  • 2 drops 20% saline solution (or a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt)
  • 2.5 oz Dry artisanal hard cider (chilled)
  • GARNISH Fresh rosemary sprig & a thin wheel of crisp green apple

METHOD

  1. Combine the gin, elderflower liqueur, lemon juice, honey syrup, and saline solution in a cocktail shaker with ice.
  2. Shake briskly for 8 to 10 seconds until well chilled.
  3. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass.
  4. Top gently with the chilled dry hard cider and give one brief, gentle lift with a barspoon to integrate.
  5. Clap the rosemary sprig between your palms to release the aromatics and tuck it beside the apple wheel.

The Granite Bastion

Named for the massive stone walls of turn-of-the-century coastal forts, this is a structured, smoky-sweet sour. It uses a split base of aged rum and reposado tequila, grounded by spiced pear and a delicate float of Islay peat that smells like ocean spray and campfire.

PROFILE: Tart Velvety Orchard-Spiced Lingering Smoke

ELEMENTS IN THE GLASS

  • Aged Column-Still Rum
  • Reposado Tequila
  • St. George Spiced Pear Liqueur
  • Laphroaig 10 Islay Scotch Float
  • Fresh Lime Juice
  • Dark Amber Maple Syrup
  • Angostura Bitters
  • Garnish: Dehydrated Lime & Fresh Nutmeg

INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING

  • 1.25 oz Aged column-still rum (Flor de Caña 7 or Bacardi 8)
  • 0.75 oz Reposado tequila
  • 0.5 oz St. George Spiced Pear Liqueur
  • 0.75 oz Fresh lime juice
  • 0.25 oz Grade A dark amber maple syrup
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • 0.25 oz Laphroaig 10 (or heavily peated Islay Scotch, reserved for floating)
  • GARNISH Dehydrated lime wheel & a grate of fresh nutmeg

METHOD

  1. Add the rum, tequila, pear liqueur, lime juice, maple syrup, and bitters to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 seconds to achieve proper dilution and a tight froth.
  3. Strain over a large clear ice cube in a rocks glass.
  4. Slowly pour the peated Scotch over the back of a barspoon so it settles as an aromatic layer across the top.
  5. Float the dehydrated lime wheel and grate a light dusting of fresh nutmeg directly over the foam.

The Parapet Nightcap

A spirit-forward, stirred closer designed for late October evenings. Straight rye whiskey and bonded apple brandy form a sturdy backbone, while Cynar and sweet vermouth bring bittersweet herbal depth, accented by roasted walnut and espresso notes.

PROFILE: Rich Warming Bittersweet Woody

ELEMENTS IN THE GLASS

  • 100-Proof Straight Rye Whiskey
  • Laird's Bonded Apple Brandy
  • Cynar Artichoke Amaro
  • Carpano Antica Sweet Vermouth
  • Nocino Green Walnut (or Espresso Liqueur)
  • Black Walnut Bitters
  • Garnish: Expressed Orange Peel & Amarena Cherry

INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING

  • 1.25 oz High-rye bourbon or 100-proof straight rye whiskey
  • 0.75 oz Laird's Straight Apple Brandy (Bottled in Bond)
  • 0.5 oz Cynar (artichoke amaro)
  • 0.5 oz Carpano Antica Formula sweet vermouth
  • 1 barspoon (0.15 oz) Nocino (green walnut liqueur) (or Borghetti espresso liqueur)
  • 2 dashes Black walnut bitters
  • GARNISH Wide swath of orange peel & a skewered Amarena cherry

METHOD

  1. Combine all liquid ingredients in a mixing glass filled two-thirds of the way with cracked ice.
  2. Stir smoothly for 30 to 35 seconds until the glass is frosted and the texture becomes silky.
  3. Strain into a chilled Nick and Nora glass.
  4. Express the oils from the orange peel generously over the surface of the drink, rub the peel along the rim and stem, then discard the peel and set the skewered cherry across the rim.

Smoky Harvest Apple Cider Margarita

A cozy autumn take on the classic margarita that balances peaty mezcal smoke against crisp spiced apple cider, tart lime, and rich pure maple syrup. Finished with fresh rosemary and a smoldering cinnamon stick.

PROFILE: Smoky Spiced Orchard Crisp Citrus Warming
ELEMENTS IN THE GLASS
  • Mezcal Joven
  • Orange Liqueur (Cointreau)
  • Fresh Apple Cider
  • Fresh Lime Juice
  • Pure Maple Syrup
  • Salted Cinnamon Sugar Rim
  • Garnish: Apple Slices, Fresh Rosemary & Smoking Cinnamon Stick
INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING
  • 2 oz Mezcal (smoky joven espadín)
  • 0.5 oz Orange liqueur (Cointreau or Triple Sec)
  • 4 oz Fresh apple cider (1/2 cup)
  • 0.67 oz Fresh lime juice (juice of 1/2 lime)
  • 1.5 tsp Pure maple syrup (1–2 tsp to taste)
  • RIM Salted cinnamon sugar rim (equal parts cinnamon sugar and sea salt)
  • GARNISH Fresh apple slices, rosemary sprig & smoking cinnamon stick
METHOD
  1. Run a lime wedge around the rim of a double rocks glass and roll in equal parts cinnamon sugar and flaky sea salt.
  2. Combine the mezcal, orange liqueur, apple cider, fresh lime juice, and maple syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice.
  3. Shake vigorously for 10 to 12 seconds until frosty and well-chilled.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice.
  5. Garnish with crisp apple slices and a rosemary sprig, then light the tip of a cinnamon stick until smoldering before placing atop the glass.

Amaretto Sour

The celebrated modern revival of the overly cloying 1970s dive staple: pairing genuine Italian amaretto with 100-proof bottled-in-bond apple brandy to provide sturdy alcoholic backbone, balanced with tart fresh lemon and silky egg white foam.

PROFILE: Toasted Almond Baked Apple Rich Citrus Silky Foam
ELEMENTS IN THE GLASS
  • Lazzaroni Amaretto
  • Laird's Straight Apple Brandy (BIB 100 Proof)
  • Fresh Lemon Juice
  • Rich Demerara Syrup (2:1)
  • Fresh Egg White
  • Garnish: Brandied Cocktail Cherry
INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING
  • 1.5 oz Lazzaroni Amaretto
  • 0.75 oz Laird’s Straight Apple Brandy (Bottled-in-Bond 100 proof)
  • 1 oz Fresh lemon juice
  • 0.25 oz Rich Demerara syrup (2:1 sugar to water)
  • 0.5 oz Fresh egg white
  • GARNISH Brandied cocktail cherry on a pick
METHOD
  1. Add amaretto, bonded apple brandy, fresh lemon juice, Demerara syrup, and egg white into a shaker tin without ice.
  2. Dry shake vigorously for 10 seconds to aerate and emulsify the egg white.
  3. Add plenty of cracked ice and shake hard for another 12 seconds until frosty and cold.
  4. Strain into an Old Fashioned rocks glass over a single large ice block.
  5. Garnish with a premium brandied cocktail cherry resting gently in the foam.

The Witcher

A brooding, cinematic cocktail combining rich bourbon with bittersweet Aperol, orange Cointreau, and an absinthe wash. Served in a walnut-smoked glass with a brandied cherry dripping dark syrup for a theatrical 'blood' effect.

PROFILE: Bitter Orange Walnut Smoke Warm Oak Anise Whisper

  • Bourbon Whiskey
  • Cointreau Orange Liqueur
  • Aperol Bittersweet Aperitif
  • Absinthe Wash
  • Peychaud's Bitters
  • Walnut Wood Smoke
  • Garnish: Brandied Cherry with Dripping 'Blood' Syrup
INGREDIENTS & PROPORTIONS 1 SERVING
  • 1.5 oz Bourbon whiskey
  • 1 oz Cointreau

  • GARNISH Hearty fresh mint bouquet sprig
METHOD
  1. Place the fresh mint leaves and 0.5 tsp sugar into a pewter or silver julep cup.
  2. Muddle gently to dissolve the sugar and release essential mint oils without tearing or shredding the leaves.
  3. Add the bourbon and simple syrup.
  4. Fill the cup three-quarters full with crushed pebble ice and stir vigorously with a barspoon until the exterior becomes completely frosted.
  5. Top with additional crushed ice to form a snowy mound.
  6. Slap a generous mint bouquet against the back of your hand to release aromatics, tuck alongside the straw, and serve.
